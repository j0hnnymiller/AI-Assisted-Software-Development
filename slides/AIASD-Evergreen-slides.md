## 🏗️ What is Legacy Code?

- Not greenfield, not evergreen
- Codebases degrade over time due to:
  - Technology shifts
  - Practices & people
  - Business rules & workflows
  - Architecture changes

<!-- _speaker: (7–10 min) Define legacy code clearly, give relatable examples. -->

---

## 🌱 What is Evergreen Code?

- Actively resists technical debt
- Secure, releasable, maintainable
- If rewritten today, it would look the same
- Evergreen is the **goal state**

<!-- _speaker: (10–13 min) Contrast evergreen with legacy, emphasize aspirational nature. -->

---

## 💡 Respect Legacy Code

- It works — don't break it
- Tested by users in production
- “You get what you get and you don't throw a fit”

<!-- _speaker: (13–15 min) Light moment: legacy code deserves respect, not fear. -->

## ⚙️ Managing AI Assistance

- Treat Copilot as a **junior developer**
- Clear instructions + careful review = success
- Strategies before engaging AI:
  - Rollback plan
  - Testing confidence
  - Peer/AI/static reviews
  - Small, incremental changes

<!-- _speaker: (15–20 min) Explain Copilot management, emphasize guardrails and discipline. -->

---

## 🧩 Context Management

- Importance of context: too little vs too much
- Prompt design: short, modular, step-by-step
- Instruction files: project rules, standards, practices
- Custom chat modes for productivity

<!-- _speaker: (20–23 min) Share practical prompt engineering tips, keep examples simple. -->

---

## 🛡️ Evergreen Practices

- Automate dependency updates (Dependabot, SCA)
- Merge small, frequent changes
- Pin & monitor dependencies
- CI checklist: green build, security review, changelog

<!-- _speaker: (23–27 min) Highlight evergreen checklist, connect to real-world workflows. -->

---

## 🧰 Safety Nets

- Linting + AI-assisted rules
- Test automation: unit, integration, E2E
- Generate tests with Copilot
- Review with multiple models
